KEY CONTRAST SUMMARY: FDA-OPT vs. STANDARD RoLoRA vs. FDA-RoLoRA
================================================================

1. PARAMETER ARENA
------------------
* FDA-Opt (fda_opt.pdf):
  Macro-space (10⁷–10⁸ weights). Full RoBERTa/DeBERTa backbone parameter space.

* Standard RoLoRA (rolora_verr4.pdf):
  Micro-subspace (∼ 1.5 × 10⁴ weights). Rank r = 4 adapter coordinates.

* FDA-RoLoRA (Our Work):
  Micro-subspace (∼ 1.5 × 10⁴ weights). Rank r = 4 adapter coordinates.


2. VARIANCE VALUES & BEHAVIOR
-----------------------------
* FDA-Opt (fda_opt.pdf):
  Smooth & Diffusive (ν ∝ e). High-dimensional statistical concentration ensures minimal stochastic jitter round-to-round.

* Standard RoLoRA (rolora_verr4.pdf):
  Unobserved & Blind. Variance is completely unmonitored during training; fixed epochs (E = 5, 10, 20) ignore real-time client drift.

* FDA-RoLoRA (Our Work):
  Volatile & Superlinear (ν ∝ e^{1.5-2.0}). Confining optimization to few coordinates yields high stochastic sensitivity; drift accelerates rapidly under non-IID partitions.


3. FACTOR SYMMETRY
------------------
* FDA-Opt (fda_opt.pdf):
  Isotropic & Symmetric. All parameters across all layers are updated simultaneously every communication round.

* Standard RoLoRA (rolora_verr4.pdf):
  Asymmetric & Alternating. Alternates between Matrix A and Matrix B; client drift compounds while the alternate factor is frozen.

* FDA-RoLoRA (Our Work):
  Dual-Track Asymmetry. Up-projection Matrix B exhibits substantially higher gradient variance than Matrix A; requires independent threshold tracks (Θ_A, Θ_B) and Matrix B variance discounting (γ = 3.0).


4. ROLE OF VARIANCE
-------------------
* FDA-Opt (fda_opt.pdf):
  Proportional Thermostat. Regulates local step duration to maintain an artificial, symmetric 50% duty cycle around the midpoint (k = 0.5).

* Standard RoLoRA (rolora_verr4.pdf):
  Passive Vulnerability. Blind accumulation of variance leads to catastrophic collapse on difficult non-IID benchmarks (e.g. MNLI C = 50 crashing to 35.66%).

* FDA-RoLoRA (Our Work):
  Adaptive Safety Valve. Threshold (κ = 1.0) acts as an asymmetric emergency brake: harvests deep local computation (13–22 epochs) during aligned phases, but truncates rounds dynamically when turbulent drift threatens divergence.
