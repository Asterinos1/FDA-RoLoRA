## 2. Round Budget (R) Discrepancies & Early Termination Audit

In several benchmark cells, runs terminated at different round horizons:

1. **Non-IID QNLI C=50, k=1.0**: Executed **13 rounds** (`R_executed = 13.0`), terminating at round 13 because validation accuracy dropped to 49.51% (chance level), tripping early stopping criteria (`ES0.8-MR18-P5-D0.005-W5`). In contrast, RoLoRA E=10 executed all 30 rounds. The thesis erroneously claimed a 56.6% bandwidth saving from this failure.

2. **Non-IID MNLI C=50, k=1.0**: Executed **22.5 rounds** on average (Seed 42: 24 rounds, Seed 43: 21 rounds) due to early stopping after peaking at round 20, whereas RoLoRA E=10 executed all 30 rounds.

3. **Non-IID QNLI C=20, E1TO40 variants**: Seed 42 ran with $R=50$, whereas Seed 43 ran with configured $R=35$ (`-R35-` in filename) and halted at round 24/30.

4. **RoLoRA E=20 on QNLI C=20 Non-IID**: Seed 42 ran with configured $R=50$ and halted at round 30, whereas Seed 43 ran with configured $R=35$ and halted at round 18.

5. **Single Seed Anomaly on QNLI C=50 RoLoRA E=20**: Only a single seed (`S42`, 30 rounds) was executed, resulting in a reported ± 0.00 standard deviation in Table 5.3.


## 3. Communication Accounting Corrections

1. **SST-2 Overcounting**: In both `update_benchmark_suite.py` and the thesis, SST-2 communication payload was computed using the 5-layer parameter rate (240 KB per client per round, or 4.6875 MB / 11.71875 MB per cohort round). Because SST-2 injects adapters into only 3 layers (6 active modules), its true bi-directional payload is **144 KB per client per round** (2.8125 MB per cohort round for C=20, 7.03125 MB for C=50). All SST-2 MB totals must be adjusted downward by 40%.

2. **Intermediate Epoch Monitoring Traffic**: Evaluating exact subspace variance requires communicating local drifts. Section 3.5 claims monitoring is governed by 'scalar norm checks', which is theoretically impossible since $\text{Var}(\Delta)$ requires the norm of the average drift $\|\frac{1}{C}\sum \Delta_i\|^2$. If AMS sketches (6 KB) are sent at intermediate epochs, total payload increases by 2.5%--5.8%; if full low-rank tensors (120 KB / 72 KB) are sent, monitoring payload roughly scales with cumulative local epochs.


## 4. Evaluation Figures Audit

1. **Figures 5.1--5.8 (IID Telemetry Stacked Plots)**: The current PNG files in `thesis/figures/` were generated by `export_individual_plots.py`, which used old `fda-standard` runs (`E3TO20`, `E3TO30`, `E3TO40`, `RK8`). They do not show the FDA-RoLoRA (Pure) method evaluated in the thesis text and tables.

2. **Figures 5.9 & 5.10 (Non-IID MNLI and QNLI Telemetry)**: Correctly depict FDA-RoLoRA Pure, but required legend patching from 'FDA Pure' to 'FDA-RoLoRA'.

3. **Figures 5.11 & 5.12 (Cross-Task Dynamics)**: Only include MNLI and QNLI panels, but the thesis text and captions call them a '4-task diagnostic synthesis'. Captions must be corrected to state MNLI and QNLI.

4. **Figure 5.13 (Dual Frontier Curve)**: The TikZ diagram depicts $\bar{e}$ high in IID and low in Non-IID, which directly contradicts all empirical data where Non-IID $\bar{e} >$ IID $\bar{e}$. The diagram must be redesigned to accurately reflect empirical setpoint tracking.
